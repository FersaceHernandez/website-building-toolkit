# Local-business landing page template

Use this as a content and implementation outline. Replace every placeholder with verified client-approved information.

1. Hero: specific service, service area, proof point, and primary CTA.
2. Trust: reviews, certifications, years in business, or project proof.
3. Services: concise service cards linked to useful detail.
4. Process: three to five steps from inquiry to completion.
5. Proof: real photos and attributable testimonials only.
6. Service area: clear locations without inventing coverage.
7. FAQ: answer the objections that prevent contact.
8. Contact: accessible form, phone link, hours, and response expectation.

Avoid fabricated reviews, unsupported guarantees, stock photos presented as client work, and unverified business details.
